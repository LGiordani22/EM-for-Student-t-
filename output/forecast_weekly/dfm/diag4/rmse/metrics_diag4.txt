error in BEA percentage points (nowcast - realised)

distance : RMSE, MAE, Bias.  RMSE_rel_ar2 < 1 beats the AR(2) benchmark.
direction: MDA     = gets the direction of change vs the last published GDP
           SignAcc = gets the sign of growth (expansion vs contraction)
           0.50 = coin toss, 1.00 = always right; n_dir = judgeable rows

TWO SAMPLES, SIDE BY SIDE — READ THEM AS A PAIR
  n     / RMSE     / MDA       every point THAT method scored
  n_com / RMSE_com / MDA_com   only the (quarter, week) points scored by
                               EVERY method in this table
Only the `_com` columns compare across rows.  The free columns can differ
in sample size — a method that starts later, or stops at week 13 instead
of 17, is averaged over a different set of targets — so a row with a
smaller `n` may look better for reasons that have nothing to do with the
model.  The gap between RMSE and RMSE_com measures exactly that.

READING ACROSS WINDOWS: raw RMSE is NOT comparable between periods — a
harder period raises it for everyone.  Compare methods WITHIN a window,
or use RMSE_rel_ar2, which divides by the benchmark of that same period.
